HDFC Tata Neu Credit Card Tracker

Track every card cycle in one place

  1. Click Import statement and pick your encrypted HDFC Tata Neu statement PDF.
  2. The app unlocks it with the password you type (kept on this device only), reads every line and verifies the printed amounts and NeuCoins against the confirmed formulas.
  3. Log each spend in the Ledger as you make it (UPI / Grocery / Base / Tata / NoCoins / Payment).
  4. Reconcile each statement to see what matched and what didn’t — plus expected vs. earned NeuCoins.
